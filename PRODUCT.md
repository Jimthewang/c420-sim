# C420 Free Sail

## Register
Product: a playable browser sailing simulator.

## People and purpose
A college sailor checks credible sailing behavior; a newcomer learns steering and sheet control. Phase 1 is free sailing only. The user-provided SPEC.md is the approved product brief and takes precedence over this summary.

## Experience
The player sits at a laptop in daylight, concentrating on wind and boat response. Clear, small, high-contrast instruments leave most of the screen to the water. A simple launch overlay introduces the two main controls. No marketing page, decorative dashboard, or cartoon graphics.

## Principles
- Every visible boat motion follows simulation state; no hidden target-speed steering.
- Explain actions in sailing language and make keyboard controls discoverable.
- Distinguish the playable Phase 1 model from later realism features.
- HTML controls remain keyboard-accessible; readable text, visible focus, reduced decorative motion.
