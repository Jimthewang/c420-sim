# Visual system

Natural daylight on a blue-green open-water training area. White stitched sails, slim aluminum spars, a white hull with a narrow dark gunwale, a visible cockpit, and a restrained wake. The camera gives the rig and water enough space to read.

The small HTML instrument panels use white/near-white surfaces with dark ink and a restrained olive primary action, derived from the design skill's seed. Alert colors have a functional purpose. System sans-serif, tabular instrument numbers, 6–10 px corner radii, no decorative card grids or heavy shadows.

Top left: product identity and free-sail state. Top right: wind compass. Bottom left: speed/heading. Bottom center: trim control and brief keys. Bottom right: camera, settings, help. Detailed physics is hidden behind F3. Help and pause appear only when requested. At narrow widths controls reflow; keyboard desktop play remains the supported input.
