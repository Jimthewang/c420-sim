# Approved visual system — October 2026 overhaul

The latest user brief replaces the original naturalistic rendering direction. The game uses a saturated miniature sailing diorama: curved C420 models, three-band toon lighting, welded inverted-hull black outlines, faceted three-dimensional islands, moving polygonal water, and stylized crew.

Palette: ink #141414; cream hulls and interface #FFFDF7; lemon beaches and primary actions #FFE144; apple meadows and normal states #5CE67C; turquoise rock #28B5A8; cyan sea #2BC4FF; pure white foam and snow #FFFFFF; coral accents and warnings #FF6B6B; teal camera controls #2EC4B6.

All main interface surfaces have a 3px ink border and a 5px hard offset shadow. Buttons move -1px with a 6px shadow on hover, and 3px with a 1px shadow on press. Bold system sans-serif, tabular instruments, solid color blocks, visible keyboard focus, no frosted cards. The world remains visible behind bounded setup, pause, and results panels.

Top left: identity and appearance controls (ink, new islands, sun hour). Top right: wind compass. Top center: race clock and status. Bottom left: speed and heading. Bottom center: mainsheet. Bottom right: camera modes, recenter, tuning, help, pause. Panels reflow at narrow widths; sailing is designed for desktop keyboard and mouse/trackpad.

Camera: 360-degree free orbit, horizontal pan, smooth 4–180m zoom, double-click/V recenter, and C cycling orbit / isometric diorama / tactical overview. Camera clearance uses the same wave and terrain height functions as the world.

Terrain visuals and grounding share a seeded four-octave continuous height field. Racing corridors stay deep. Waves drive render buoyancy and foam, while the original sailing force model, capsizes, rules, lessons, and scoring remain authoritative.
